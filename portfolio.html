About

I am a homoeopathic physician with hands-on experience in chronic disease management, individualised prescribing and long-term patient follow-up. I completed my BHMS at Father Muller Homoeopathic Medical College, Mangaluru, then added a diploma in Nutrition and Health Education and a speciality course in Predictive Homoeopathy.

At Complete Care I lead treatment planning and case reviews for complex and long-standing cases, and I mentor trainee and junior doctors. My approach looks at miasmatic evaluation, layers of suppression and the person’s full history, so each plan fits the patient in front of me.

I took part in four research projects and a dissertation during medical training, including one selected under the CCRH Short Term Studentship in Homoeopathy. Earlier work in medical writing helps me explain care plans clearly to patients and colleagues.

How I work

Treatment follows predictive homoeopathy: miasmatic evaluation, layers of suppression, embryological considerations and the theory of acutes. Cases are repertorised with Radar and Hompath, and progress is tracked with validated tools such as HAM-D, VAS and the Bates-Jensen Wound Assessment Tool where they apply.
